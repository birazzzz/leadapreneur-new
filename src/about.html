Why we exist

We built Leadapreneur to future-proof your talent.

Leadapreneur is a future-proofing company. For 20 years, across 60,000+ professionals and $100M+ in measurable innovation ROI, we have done one thing: prepare talent for what's coming next.

Our answer to the AI challenge is the AI x Talent Accelerator — a 6-month programme where participants don't just learn about AI. They use it to individually propose, build and deploy real innovation projects with measurable business impact.

The standard we hold every participant to

One deployed AI-enabled, KPI-aligned innovation project worth at least RM 40,000 within 6 months.

We've done this with DBS, CIMB, OCBC, HSBC, AmBank, RHB, AXA, Axiata, Gamuda, Astro, Bursa Malaysia, Toshiba Malaysia, AMK and dozens more across banking, insurance, manufacturing, oil and gas, and GLCs across Southeast Asia.
