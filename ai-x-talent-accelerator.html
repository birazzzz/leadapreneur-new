05 · Plan succession

The Greatness Grid

Traditional succession planning relies on manager opinions, performance reviews and subjective assessments of potential. Leadapreneur shifts that flawed model from subjective evaluations to objective valuations.

One simple principle: you earn your title by delivering value, not by being nominated for it. Three seasons, three levels of leadership. To graduate a level, a leadapreneur's project must meet the minimum valuation requirement — for example USD 10,000 for an AI Innovator.

AI Innovator

Season one. Deliver a project that clears the valuation bar.

AI Coach

Season two. Deliver again, and bring others with you.

AI Leader

Season three. Lead adoption at organisational scale.

Every step of the journey is earned, measurable and real.
